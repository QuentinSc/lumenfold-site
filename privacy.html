English

Lumenfold does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal data.

  • No data is collected and nothing is shared with anyone.
  • The app has no network access: it never goes online.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account.
  • Your settings (theme, resting darkness, breathing, animation speed) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
